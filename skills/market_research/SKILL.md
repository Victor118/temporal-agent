# Market Research Skill

## Instructions
When conducting a market research study:

### 1. Define the Scope
- Clarify the product, service, or market segment to analyze
- Identify the target geography and audience
- Determine the key questions to answer

### 2. Market Overview
- Estimate the market size (TAM, SAM, SOM)
- Identify growth trends and projections
- Map the value chain and key stakeholders
- Note relevant regulations or barriers to entry

### 3. Competitive Landscape
- Identify direct and indirect competitors
- Analyze their positioning, pricing, and distribution
- Assess strengths and weaknesses (SWOT per competitor)
- Identify market share distribution if available

### 4. Target Audience
- Define customer segments and personas
- Identify pain points and unmet needs
- Analyze buying behavior and decision criteria
- Estimate willingness to pay

### 5. Trends & Opportunities
- Highlight emerging trends (technology, regulation, consumer behavior)
- Identify gaps in the current market
- Suggest potential differentiators
- Flag risks and threats

### 6. Output Format
Structure the deliverable as:
- **Executive Summary** : key findings in 3-5 bullet points
- **Market Overview** : size, growth, dynamics
- **Competitive Analysis** : comparison matrix + detailed profiles
- **Customer Insights** : segments, needs, behaviors
- **Opportunities & Risks** : ranked by impact and likelihood
- **Recommendations** : actionable next steps with prioritization

### Guidelines
- Use data and sources whenever possible, cite them
- Distinguish between facts and estimates/assumptions
- Be specific with numbers: prefer ranges over vague qualifiers
- Adapt the depth of analysis to the maturity of the market (emerging vs. established)
- If information is unavailable, state it explicitly rather than guessing
